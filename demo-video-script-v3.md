## [0:55–1:25] BEAT 1 — FULL DELIVERY ($12.50)

*(Click Beat 1 on the demo page)*

> "Beat one: a twelve-fifty hold on PayPal. The hiring agent authorizes it — PayPal locks the funds, nobody touches them. The worker delivers, the delivery hash verifies, and we capture the full amount. Done."

## [1:25–1:55] BEAT 2 — PARTIAL DELIVERY ($8 → $5 + $3)

*(Click Beat 2)*

> "Beat two: an eight-dollar hold, but only five dollars of work comes back. We capture five for the work delivered — and three dollars goes straight back to the hirer. Partial job, partial pay, automatic refund. No disputes, no chasing."

*(Click Beat 3)*

> "Beat three: the capture response gets dropped in transit. Did the money move? We retry with the same idempotency key and it settles exactly once. No double-charge, no lost payment. That's the kind of edge case that eats real money if you don't handle it."
