# Lamplight Checkout — Demo Video Script v2
**Target: under 3 minutes. Tone: creative but professional.**

---

## [0:00–0:20] THE PROBLEM

*(Open on the demo page, or a simple title card)*

> "AI agents are starting to hire each other. One agent needs a delivery run, another needs data labeled, a third needs a design draft.
>
> But here's the problem: how does the hiring agent pay without getting burned? Send the money first and the job might never get done. Do the work first and you might never get paid.
>
> Trust shouldn't be the price of doing business."

---

## [0:20–0:40] THE SOLUTION

> "Lamplight Checkout is a PayPal-powered conditional payment coordination platform for AI agents.
>
> An agent can hire another agent. The money stays held on PayPal until the delivery matches — and a partial job pays partial and gives the rest back.
>
> PayPal holds the funds. We coordinate authorize, verify delivery, then capture. Three beats, live on the mock sandbox."

---

## [0:40–1:20] BEAT 1 — FULL DELIVERY ($12.50)

*(Click Beat 1 on the demo page)*

> "Beat one: a $12.50 hold. The hiring agent authorizes the payment — PayPal locks it, nobody touches it. The worker delivers. Delivery verified, we capture the full amount. Done."

---

## [1:20–2:00] BEAT 2 — PARTIAL DELIVERY ($8 → $5 + $3)

*(Click Beat 2)*

> "Beat two: an $8 hold, but only $5 of work gets done. The hiring agent captures $5 for the work delivered — and the remaining $3 goes straight back. Partial job, partial pay, automatic refund. No disputes, no chasing."

---

## [2:00–2:40] BEAT 3 — DROPPED RESPONSE, IDEMPOTENT RETRY

*(Click Beat 3)*

> "Beat three: the capture response gets dropped in transit. The money's in limbo — did it go through? We retry with the same idempotency key, and it settles exactly once. No double-charge, no lost payment."

---

## [2:40–2:56] CLOSE

> "Lamplight Checkout. Trust shouldn't be the price of doing business.
>
> Built for the PayPal AI Hackathon. Links in the description."
